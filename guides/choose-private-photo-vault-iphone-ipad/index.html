Choosing a private photo vault for iPhone or iPad? This guide compares Freezon, Apple’s Hidden album, Private Photo Vault, Keepsafe, SPV – Secret Photo Vault, Secret Photo Album – SA, PV – Secret Photo Album, Calculator#, and Safe Lock. See which options offer separate passwords, on-device encryption, backups, and ad-free use, and what they cost.

If you just want a few pictures out of your main library, the Hidden album in Apple Photos is enough. For photos and videos you want behind a separate password, try Freezon. Enter a different password to open a different album. Every imported file is encrypted on your device, and encrypted backups are available in the free, ad-free app.

Sharing an iPad? Keep two albums behind two passwords

A family iPad often has a passcode everyone knows. Apple’s Hidden album uses device authentication. In Freezon, keep the photos you reach for often in your Everyday album and put anything you want to lock separately in your Private album. Both open from the same screen: the password you enter decides which photos you see.

Use Face ID or Touch ID for the Everyday album if you like. The Private album has its own password. On either iPhone or iPad, imported photos and videos are available offline.

Changing phones? Take your encrypted backup with you

If you prefer cloud backups, look at Keepsafe’s Private Cloud or PV’s cloud sync. With Freezon, each album can have its own encrypted backup file. Save it where you want using the Files app, then select it on your new device and enter that album’s recovery code.

Import photos and videos directly from Apple Photos into Freezon. After import, you can keep the originals in Photos or follow the prompt to remove them. Moving from another vault? Export the items you want to Apple Photos first, then import them into Freezon.

Two albums for free, more room with Pro

Freezon’s free version gives you two albums with separate passwords and encrypted backups, with no ads. When you need more room, a one-time Pro purchase removes the limits for both albums.
