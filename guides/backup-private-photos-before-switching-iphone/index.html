Backing Up and Migrating Private Photos Before Switching iPhones

Safely back up and migrate your Freezon encrypted photo vault to a new iPhone before wiping your old device.

Pre-Migration Checklist

Moving to a new iPhone requires deliberate preparation for your private photos. Freezon is an on-device encrypted photo vault for iPhone and iPad that neither uploads albums to developers nor syncs them automatically across devices. You cannot assume standard Apple system migration will transfer your Freezon albums. Before switching, follow this checklist:

  • Create an encrypted backup on your old device and verify completion in the app.
  • Ensure your new device can access the backup file, and safely store the matching security recovery code.
  • Restore the album from the backup during setup on the new device.
  • Confirm all content is readable on your new device before wiping or clearing your old device.

Creating and Saving the Backup

Daily albums and private albums manage their backups and recovery codes independently. Open the album you wish to migrate and use the encrypted backup feature to select an accessible folder through the system Files app, such as a directory in iCloud Drive. While iCloud Drive can sync the backup file across devices, this file transfer is distinct from automatic album synchronization. Always check inside Freezon to confirm the backup has completed, and save the corresponding security recovery code in a secure location.

Restoring on Your New iPhone

When setting up your album in Freezon on the new iPhone, choose the encrypted backup option and enter the matching security recovery code. You will then set a password for the restored album. Finally, open the album to verify that your photos are readable and intact before handling, wiping, or erasing your old device.
